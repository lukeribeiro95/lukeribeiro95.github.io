How a video is made

  1. Research and a fact-checked script in Portuguese.
  2. Narration with text-to-speech, checked word by word with speech recognition.
  3. Original characters and scenes animated in code, rendered frame by frame.
  4. Synthesized score and sound effects, mixed to streaming loudness (−14 LUFS).
  5. Burned-in captions, review, and publishing.
